Conclusion
The first IDS simultaneously addressing cross-layer correlation, marketplace domain specificity, and full SHAP explainability. After 30 years of IDS research, the remaining frontier is architectural — no published system fuses network and application behavioral signals for a marketplace threat model. SoukDZ fills that gap with a deployable, explainable, production-ready architecture.
